*   **Honda Activa (6G & 125):** The Activa 6G comes with a refined 110cc engine, known for its smooth performance and fuel efficiency. The Activa 125 offers a slightly more powerful 125cc engine, providing better acceleration for city traffic and occasional highway rides. Both are famous for their Honda reliability and low maintenance. The Activa 125's engine feels peppier, which is appreciated on slightly longer commutes.
*   **TVS Jupiter:** The Jupiter features a 110cc engine that focuses on smooth power delivery and good low-end torque, making it comfortable for city riding. It often boasts features like an external fuel filler cap and a silent start system, which are convenient.
*   **Suzuki Access 125:** This scooter is known for its strong 125cc engine, which delivers brisk acceleration and a good top speed. If performance is a top priority, the Access 125 is often a strong contender, offering a lively ride experience.
