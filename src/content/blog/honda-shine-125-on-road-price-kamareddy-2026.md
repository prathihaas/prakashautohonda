## What Makes Up the On-Road Price of Your Honda Shine 125?

Let's break down the components that contribute to the final Honda Shine 125 on-road price in Kamareddy:

### Ex-showroom Price
This is the manufacturer's price without any taxes, registration, or insurance. It's the base cost of the bike itself.

### RTO (Road Transport Office) Registration Charges
In Telangana, RTO charges are a percentage of the ex-showroom price and vary based on the vehicle's cost. This fee registers your Honda Shine 125 with the government, making it legal to ride on public roads in Kamareddy, Banswada, and across the state. These charges cover your number plate, smart card registration, and road tax.

### Insurance Costs
Mandatory by law, third-party insurance covers damages to another vehicle or person in an accident. You can opt for a comprehensive policy that also covers damages to your own Honda Shine 125. The cost depends on the type of policy (third-party vs. comprehensive) and its duration (typically 1 year or 5 years for third-party). We help you choose the best plan for your needs and budget right here at Prakash Auto Honda.

### Handling Charges / Logistics
While some dealers might list these separately, at Prakash Auto Honda, we aim to be transparent. These are minimal costs associated with transporting the bike from the factory to our showroom in Kamareddy and preparing it for delivery.

### Optional Accessories & Extended Warranty
