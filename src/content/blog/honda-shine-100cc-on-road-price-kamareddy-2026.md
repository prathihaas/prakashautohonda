### What Influences the On-Road Price of the Honda Shine 100cc?

Several factors contribute to the final on-road cost of this popular commuter bike:

*   **Ex-showroom Price:** This is the manufacturer's suggested retail price, excluding taxes and other charges. It's the base price of the motorcycle.
*   **RTO Registration:** This is a mandatory charge for registering your vehicle with the Telangana Transport Department. It includes road tax and other fees, which vary based on the vehicle's price and engine capacity. For 100cc bikes, these charges are generally lower than for higher-capacity models.
*   **Insurance:** A third-party insurance policy is legally required, and many buyers opt for a comprehensive policy for better coverage. The premium depends on the type of policy, the insurer, and the bike's value. We can help you choose the best insurance plan.
*   **Optional Accessories:** While not part of the mandatory on-road price, many customers choose to add accessories like seat covers, crash guards, or helmet locks, which will add to the total cost.
