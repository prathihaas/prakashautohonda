## Breaking Down the Activa 110 cc On Road Price in Kamareddy (2026)

Let's meticulously break down what contributes to the overall cost of your new Activa 6G 110cc scooter in Kamareddy. This comprehensive breakdown ensures there are no surprises when you visit our showroom.

### Ex-Showroom Price

The ex-showroom price is the base cost of the vehicle, set by Honda. This price does not include taxes, registration, or insurance. For the Activa 6G 110cc, this price typically varies slightly between different variants (Standard, Deluxe, H-Smart). This is the starting point for all calculations.

### Road Tax & Registration (RTO Charges)

This is a significant component of the on-road price and is mandated by the Telangana Transport Department. The Road Tax is calculated as a percentage of the ex-showroom price. For two-wheelers in Telangana, this percentage can vary based on the vehicle's cost. Additionally, there are charges for the registration certificate, number plate, and other administrative fees. These charges are specific to the Kamareddy RTO.

### Insurance Cost

Every new vehicle sold in India must have at least third-party insurance. Most buyers opt for comprehensive insurance, which covers damages to your own vehicle as well. The insurance premium depends on several factors:

*   **Type of coverage:** Third-party vs. Comprehensive.
*   **Insured Declared Value (IDV):** The maximum sum assured by the insurer.
*   **Add-ons:** Such as zero depreciation, roadside assistance, etc.

We work with leading insurance providers to get you competitive quotes for your new scooter.

### Other Charges

Beyond the main components, there are usually a few other minor charges:

*   **Handling Charges:** For logistics and preparation of the vehicle.
*   **Extended Warranty (Optional):** Many customers choose to extend their warranty for peace of mind.
*   **Accessories (Optional):** Things like seat covers, floor mats, guards, and other genuine Honda accessories.
*   **Helmet:** Often included as a mandatory item or offered as a package.
