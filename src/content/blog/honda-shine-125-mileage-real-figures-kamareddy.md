## Factors Affecting Your Honda Shine 125 Mileage

While 55-60 kmpl is a great benchmark, several factors can influence your actual mileage. Understanding these can help you squeeze every last drop of efficiency from your Honda Shine 125:

### Riding Style and Conditions

Aggressive riding, sudden acceleration, and hard braking consume more fuel. Smooth, consistent riding at moderate speeds (typically 40-60 kmph) will always yield better mileage. Riding in heavy city traffic with frequent stops and starts will naturally result in lower figures compared to cruising on an open road between, say, Kamareddy and Banswada.

### Maintenance and Servicing

Regular servicing is paramount. A well-maintained engine with clean air filters, properly adjusted chain tension, and fresh engine oil performs optimally. Skipping service intervals can significantly reduce your Honda Shine 125 mileage. At Prakash Auto Honda, we emphasize timely service to all our customers for this very reason.

### Tire Pressure

Underinflated tires increase rolling resistance, forcing the engine to work harder and consume more fuel. Always maintain the recommended tire pressure for both front and rear wheels. This is a simple check that can make a noticeable difference.

### Load and Terrain

Carrying heavier loads or riding uphill frequently will naturally demand more power from the engine, impacting fuel efficiency. Kamareddy's relatively flat terrain is generally favorable for good mileage, but if you frequently travel with a pillion or heavy luggage, expect a slight dip.
