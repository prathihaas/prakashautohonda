## Understanding the Honda Dio's On-Road Cost in Kamareddy

When you're looking at the cost of a new scooter, you'll often see two main figures: the ex-showroom price and the on-road price. The ex-showroom price is what the manufacturer charges for the vehicle itself, excluding taxes, registration, and insurance. The on-road price, which is what you'll actually pay, includes several additional components.

Here in Kamareddy, the on-road price for this popular scooter includes:

### RTO Charges (Road Tax & Registration)

These are mandatory charges levied by the Regional Transport Office for vehicle registration. In Telangana, these vary based on the vehicle's cost.

### Insurance

Comprehensive insurance is compulsory for new vehicles. The cost depends on the insured declared value (IDV) of the scooter and the type of coverage.

### Handling Charges/Logistics

Minor charges for transporting the vehicle to the dealership.

### Accessories (Optional)

Any additional accessories you choose to add, like seat covers, guards, or a helmet.
