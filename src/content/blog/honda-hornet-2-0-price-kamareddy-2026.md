### Breaking Down the On-Road Cost of the Hornet 2.0

The on-road cost for any two-wheeler, including this Honda model, is a sum of several components:

1.  **Ex-Showroom Price:** This is the base price set by Honda. It's consistent across dealers in a state but doesn't include taxes or registration.
2.  **RTO Charges (Registration & Road Tax):** In Telangana, these charges are a percentage of the ex-showroom price and vary based on the vehicle's cost. This is a significant portion of the on-road price.
3.  **Insurance:** Mandatory third-party insurance covers damages to third parties. Comprehensive insurance, which also covers damage to your own vehicle, is highly recommended. The premium depends on the chosen coverage and insurer.
4.  **Handling Charges/Logistics:** Minor charges sometimes included for transportation and dealer services.
5.  **Optional Accessories:** Things like crash guards, seat covers, or special graphics can add to the final cost.

At our Kamareddy showroom, we provide a transparent breakdown of all these costs so you know exactly what you're paying for. We've noticed that for the Hornet 2.0, many customers from Machareddy and Domakonda often inquire about comprehensive insurance for peace of mind, which is a wise choice.
