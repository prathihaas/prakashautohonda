## Factors that affect your bike EMI

Several variables affect the final EMI beyond the base price. The Telangana RTO levies a road tax that varies with the vehicle’s engine capacity and price bracket; this is added to the on‑road cost. Insurance premiums depend on the model’s claim history and the coverage you choose (third‑party vs comprehensive). Our calculator pulls the latest RTO tax slabs and insurance quotes from our partner providers, so the output reflects real‑world charges.

Your credit score also plays a role. A higher score may qualify you for a lower interest rate, reducing the EMI. Conversely, a limited credit history might attract a slightly higher rate. We advise customers to check their score beforehand; if needed, we can guide you on improving it before applying.

Seasonal demand influences pricing too. During festival periods like Dasara and Sankranti, demand spikes and dealers may offer limited‑time discounts that lower the on‑road price. Conversely, after the harvest season, some models see a slight price correction. Keeping an eye on these trends can help you time your purchase for maximum savings.
