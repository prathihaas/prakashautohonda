## Engine and Performance: Which Scooter Feels More Powerful?

The Activa 6G comes with a 109.51cc fan-cooled, 4-stroke engine, known for its buttery-smooth power delivery and exceptional refinement. It produces around 7.79 PS of power and 8.79 Nm of torque. On the other hand, the Access 125 boasts a slightly larger 124cc engine, delivering about 8.7 PS of power and 10 Nm of torque.

**What does this mean for your daily ride in Kamareddy?**

*   **Activa 6G:** Its engine is incredibly smooth and quiet. For city commutes, running errands, or short trips within Kamareddy, Machareddy, or Domakonda, the Activa 6G feels perfectly adequate. The acceleration is linear and predictable, making it very easy to handle in traffic.
*   **Access 125:** The extra cubic capacity and power are noticeable, especially when you need quick overtakes or if you frequently carry a pillion. If you often ride on the highways connecting Kamareddy to other towns, or if you need to carry heavier loads, the Access 125 might feel a bit more effortless.

At our Kamareddy showroom, the question we hear most is about power for two riders. While the Access 125 has more grunt, the Activa 6G handles two people comfortably for most local use cases, thanks to its well-tuned engine and Honda's PGM-FI technology.

## Mileage: How Fuel-Efficient are They?

Fuel efficiency is a major deciding factor for scooter buyers in our region. Here's how they stack up:

*   **Honda Activa 6G Real Mileage:** Our customers consistently report real-world mileage figures between **52-57 kmpl** for the Activa 6G in mixed city and highway conditions. This is a strong point for the Honda scooter, especially with the rising fuel prices.
*   **Suzuki Access 125 Real Mileage:** The Access 125 typically delivers mileage in the range of 48-53 kmpl. While still good, it's generally a couple of kmpl less than the Activa 6G due to its larger engine.

For someone commuting daily from Yellareddy to Kamareddy, those few extra kilometers per litre from the Activa 6G can add up to significant savings over time. Honda's eSP (Enhanced Smart Power) technology, which includes an ACG starter for silent starts and an Idling Stop System, contributes significantly to its impressive fuel economy.

## Comfort and Ergonomics: Which is More Comfortable?

Both scooters offer a comfortable ride, but there are subtle differences:

*   **Seating Position:** Both have an upright, comfortable riding posture. The Access 125 often feels a bit larger, with a slightly longer and wider seat, which some riders might prefer, especially for longer durations or with a pillion.
*   **Suspension:** The Activa 6G features a telescopic front suspension and a 3-step adjustable spring-loaded hydraulic rear suspension. The Access 125 also has telescopic front forks and a swing arm type rear suspension. Both are well-suited to absorb the bumps and potholes common on our Kamareddy roads.
*   **Floorboard Space:** The Activa 6G generally offers decent floorboard space, but the Access 125 is often praised for its slightly more generous area, which can be useful for carrying groceries or a small bag.
