### Ex-Showroom Price vs. On-Road Price

The ex-showroom price is the manufacturing cost plus dealer margin and GST. This is the base price. However, to get your scooter on the road legally in Kamareddy, you'll need to account for a few more things:

*   **RTO Registration:** This is the Road Transport Office charge for registering your vehicle, allowing it to legally ply on Telangana roads. It includes road tax and other administrative fees. These vary slightly based on the scooter's value.
*   **Insurance:** Mandatory third-party insurance covers any damage or injury to a third party. Comprehensive insurance, which we highly recommend, also covers damage to your own vehicle. The premium depends on the scooter's IDV (Insured Declared Value) and the type of coverage.
*   **Handling Charges/Logistics:** These are minor charges for transporting the vehicle from the factory to our showroom and preparing it for delivery.
*   **Accessories (Optional):** Many customers opt for additional accessories like seat covers, floor mats, or side stands, which add to the total cost.
