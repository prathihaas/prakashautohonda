### Engine and Performance

The Activa 125's 124cc engine produces 8.29 bhp and 10.3 Nm torque, while the 6G's 109cc unit makes 7.79 bhp and 8.9 Nm. That extra grunt matters if you frequently carry a pillion or ride on the NH44 stretch near Kamareddy. The 125 also gets a silent start with ACG starter, which is quieter and more reliable over time.

### Mileage

Real-world mileage for the Activa 6G is 52–57 kmpl, while the Activa 125 returns 50–55 kmpl. The difference is marginal — about 2–5 kmpl — so don't let that be the deciding factor unless you ride over 50 km daily.

### Features

The Activa 125 Deluxe gets an LED headlamp, external fuel fill, and a combi-brake system (CBS) with equalizer. The 6G Deluxe has a halogen headlamp, but still offers alloy wheels and front disc brake. Both have a large under-seat storage that fits a full-size helmet.
