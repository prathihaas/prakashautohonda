The Honda Activa has been the undisputed king of scooters in India for years, and its popularity in Kamareddy is no different. ### Honda Activa 6G Mileage: Real-World Performance

The Activa 6G, with its refined 110cc engine, is designed for smooth, efficient city rides. Based on feedback from hundreds of customers and our own observations, the real-world fuel efficiency for the Activa 6G typically falls between **52-57 kmpl**. ### Honda Activa 125 Mileage: Practical Fuel Economy

The Activa 125 offers a bit more power and a slightly larger engine, which many assume would mean significantly lower fuel efficiency. However, thanks to Honda's eSP (Enhanced Smart Power) technology, the Activa 125 manages to deliver surprisingly similar real-world figures. Our customers report an average of **52-57 kmpl**, which is excellent for a 125cc scooter. The extra power is noticeable, especially with a pillion rider or on slight inclines, without a major compromise on how far you can go on a litre of petrol.

## Factors Affecting Your Scooter's Fuel Efficiency

While we provide a range for the Honda Activa's mileage, several factors can influence the actual kmpl you achieve:

### Riding Style and Road Conditions

Aggressive acceleration, sudden braking, and consistently high speeds will naturally reduce fuel economy. Smooth, consistent riding at moderate speeds yields the best results. The stop-and-go traffic often encountered near the Kamareddy bus stand or market areas will typically result in lower mileage compared to a clear stretch of road towards Yellareddy.

### Maintenance and Servicing

Regular servicing, as recommended by Honda, is crucial. A well-maintained engine, properly inflated tyres, and clean air filters all contribute significantly to optimal fuel efficiency. At Prakash Auto Honda, our service center ensures your scooter is always running at its best.

### Fuel Quality and Load

Using good quality fuel is important. Additionally, carrying heavy loads or frequently riding with a pillion can slightly impact the scooter's fuel consumption.
